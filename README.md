# Hegemonic Pay — Merchant Services, POS & Payment Processing Docs

Accepting card payments, managing counter checkouts, and running online transactions requires dependable processing infrastructure, clear settlement workflows, and responsive support. Hegemonic Pay is built to help businesses handle merchant accounts, point-of-sale terminals, and payment acceptance without juggling disconnected billing tools.

Explore the full [Hegemonic Pay Merchant Services](https://hegemonicinc.com/hegemonic-pay-merchant-services/) overview to see how merchant accounts, checkout setups, and payment options come together for your business.

## Core Merchant & Checkout Workflows

* **Merchant Account & Payment Processing:** Set up card acceptance, review underwriting requirements, and manage payment workflows tailored to your business model.
* **Counter POS & Mobile Readers:** Pair front-desk terminals and mobile card readers with in-store sales and customer billing so checkouts stay fast and accurate.
* **Online & Recurring Billing:** Support e-commerce checkouts and repeat payment flows with clear transaction records and reliable settlement.

## Gateway, Hardware & Integration Overview

For businesses connecting payment processing with point-of-sale systems, web checkouts, or back-office reporting, structured payment setup keeps reconciliation straightforward.
